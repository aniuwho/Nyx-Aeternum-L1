# Rocket Design

## Design Overview

The rocket was designed using OpenRocket, with the goal of developing a structurally sound high-power rocket capable of achieving a successful Level 1 certification flight.

The complete rocket configuration was modeled digitally before construction, including the airframe, nose cone, fin configuration, motor placement, and recovery system.

## Design Software

**OpenRocket** was used as the primary rocket design and simulation tool.

The software was used to:
- Develop a digital model of the rocket.
- Configure the airframe and major structural components.
- Evaluate aerodynamic stability using the Center of Gravity (CG) and Center of Pressure (CP).
- Estimate flight performance through simulations.
- Analyze expected parachute descent and recovery performance.

## Airframe Configuration

The rocket consisted of the following major components:

| Component | Configuration |
|---|---|
| Body Tube | Cardboard |
| Nose Cone | Plastic |
| Fins | Three laser-cut plywood fins |
| Motor Tube | Cardboard |
| Centering Rings | Three plywood rings |
| Avionics Bay | Custom 3D-printed assembly |
| Recovery System | Parachute and shock cord |

The rocket used a three-fin configuration with fin tabs extending inward toward the motor tube. The motor assembly was supported within the airframe using plywood centering rings.

## Component Layout

The overall design incorporated several major subsystems:

- **Airframe:** Main cardboard body tube and plastic nose cone.
- **Fin Assembly:** Three plywood fins integrated into the motor mount structure.
- **Motor Mount:** Cardboard motor tube supported by plywood centering rings.
- **Avionics:** Removable, custom 3D-printed avionics bay housed within the nose cone.
- **Recovery:** Parachute and shock cord configuration connecting the nose cone and main body.

The avionics bay was designed to be removable, allowing access to the flight electronics and recorded data after recovery.

## Stability and Flight Performance

OpenRocket was used to evaluate the rocket's stability and estimate its expected flight performance.

The design analysis included:

- Center of Gravity (CG) and Center of Pressure (CP) evaluation.
- Predicted apogee.
- Flight velocity throughout the trajectory.
- Estimated flight duration.
- Parachute deployment and recovery descent velocity.

These analyses provided insight into the expected behavior of the rocket throughout its flight.
