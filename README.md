# Level 1 (L1) High Power Rocketry Certification

**Mechanical Design | Structural Construction | Avionics Integration | Flight Performance**

A high-power model rocket designed using OpenRocket, constructed through a combination of conventional materials and custom-fabricated components, and successfully flown to an apogee of **1,075 ft** as part of an L1 certification project.

---

## Project Overview

This project involved the design, construction, and flight of a Level 1 high-power rocket. The rocket incorporated a cardboard airframe, laser-cut plywood structural components, a plastic nose cone, and a custom 3D-printed avionics bay.

A StratoLogger flight computer was integrated into the nose cone assembly alongside a 9V battery and screw switch to support onboard flight instrumentation.

The rocket successfully completed its flight using an H123A motor, reaching a recorded apogee of **1,075 ft**.

## Engineering & Development

### 1. Design & Simulation
Rocket design and initial flight simulations were performed using OpenRocket. The software was used during the design phase to model the rocket and evaluate its predicted flight performance.

[View Design Documentation](Design/Design.md)

### 2. Structural Construction
The rocket was assembled using a cardboard body tube, plastic nose cone, and laser-cut plywood components. The fins, motor mount, and centering rings were integrated into the airframe using epoxy.

[View Fabrication Documentation](Fabrication/Fabrication.md)

### 3. Avionics Integration
A custom 3D-printed electronics bay was designed to accommodate a StratoLogger flight computer, 9V battery, and screw switch. The assembly was integrated into the nose cone.

[View Avionics Documentation](Avionics/Avionics.md)

### 4. Recovery System
The nose cone and body tube were connected through a shock cord, with the parachute attached to the nose cone as part of the recovery assembly.

[View Recovery Documentation](Recovery/Recovery.md)

### 5. Flight Performance
The rocket successfully flew using an H123A motor and reached a recorded apogee of 1,075 ft. Flight data and performance analysis will be documented as the recorded telemetry becomes available.

[View Flight Analysis](Flight-Data/Flight-Analysis.md)
