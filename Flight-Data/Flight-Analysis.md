# Flight Performance & Analysis

## Overview

The rocket successfully completed its Level 1 high-power flight using an H123A motor, reaching a recorded apogee of **1,075 ft**.

Flight altitude was recorded by the onboard StratoLogger flight computer. The recovery system deployed successfully, and the rocket was recovered following the flight.
